      Mediclaim

      A health policy only helps if the cover fits your family and the claim goes through. We help with both.

      • Picking cover for individuals, families and senior parents
      • Understanding waiting periods, sub-limits and exclusions
      • Top-up and super top-up options on an existing policy
      • Renewal reminders and help when you need to claim

      Before you buy

      Check the room-rent limit, the waiting period for pre-existing conditions, and the insurer's claim settlement record. Ask us to walk you through them.

      Insurance

      Insurance should replace income if you can't earn it. We help you work out how much cover that takes and which plan type suits you.

      • Term insurance sized to income, loans and dependants
      • Life, vehicle, travel and personal accident cover
      • Premium calendar so a policy never lapses by accident
      • Nominee and claim paperwork guidance

      A quick rule

      Cover that is many times your yearly income is a common starting point for term insurance. Your loans and family's needs decide the real number. Ask us to work it out with you.

        Risk meter

        SEBI requires every mutual fund scheme to carry a riskometer. It has six levels, from lowest to highest risk. Check the level in the scheme's documents before you invest.

        1. Low
        2. Low to moderate
        3. Moderate
        4. Moderately high
        5. High
        6. Very high
